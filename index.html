About Us

At Funny Routes Travel, we design travel experiences that inspire people to explore the world with joy and curiosity. Our agency focuses on offering safe, creative, and memorable trips for every type of traveler. We work with good and trusted companies, and with a specialized team that makes every trip a memorable experience. Our goal is to help travelers connect with new cultures, nature, and unforgettable moments.

Vision

To become a travel agency recognized for its creativity, quality, and love for exploration. We dream of inspiring more people to travel responsibly and discover the world with open minds and hearts. At Funny Routes Travel, we want to lead a new way of tourism that values cultural exchange, environmental care, and unforgettable memories. We will keep growing with passion, innovation, and teamwork helping travelers find not just destinations, but experiences that change their lives

Mission

To create unforgettable travel experiences that make people smile, explore, and connect with the world. We want every traveler to feel the excitement of discovering new places, cultures, and traditions in a safe and fun way. Our goal is to offer personalized trips that mix adventure, relaxation, and learning, always promoting respect for nature and local communities. We believe that traveling is more than visiting destinations, it’s about living stories, meeting people, and finding joy in every journey.

Objectives

  • 1. Offer travel packages to popular and safe destinations.
  • 2. Provide good customer service before, during, and after the trip.
  • 3. Work with hotels and transport companies to give better prices.
  • 4. Use social media to promote our travel offers.
  • 5. Create unforgettable experiences for every traveler.

Goals

  • • Increase the number of clients every year.
  • • Build strong relationships with local and national tourism partners.
  • • Be recognized as a trusted travel agency in our city.
  • • Offer eco-friendly and cultural travel options.
